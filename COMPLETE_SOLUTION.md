## 📈 Sentiment Metrics

### Sentiment Score
- **Range**: -1.0 to +1.0
- **Negative**: More negative than positive tweets
- **Positive**: More positive than negative tweets
- **Example**: Trump at -0.15 = 35% positive, 46% negative

### Tweet Volume
- **Total tweets** about candidate
- **Trending**: Volume > 5,000 or increasing
- **Normal**: Regular discussion level
- **Example**: Trump at 7,000 = TRENDING

### Sentiment Breakdown
- **Positive**: % of favorable tweets
- **Negative**: % of critical tweets
- **Neutral**: % of informational tweets
